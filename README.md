## Why "Back-of-the-Napkin Math" Traps Even Smart Buyers

Most buyers evaluate a property using one static equation:
> **Expected Sale Price (or Annual Rent) − (Purchase Price + Rehab + Interest) = Profit**

If the number is positive, they buy. **In reality, profitable deals go broke every month because real estate is a moving cash-flow timeline—not a single static number.**

This workbook protects you from **four hidden blind spots** automatically—without requiring a finance degree:

1. **"Profitable on Paper, Bankrupt in Month 8" (Cash-Timing Risk):** A flip can promise $150,000 profit at Month 12 and still drain your checking account to $0 in Month 6 when contractor draws, loan payments, and delayed tax refunds collide. This model flags the exact shortfall month before you commit.
2. **"Contractor Delays Are Free" (Hidden Cost of Time):** Extending a rehab from 6 months to 10 months quietly burns extra mortgage interest, taxes, insurance, and utilities while locking up your cash. Changing the duration cell immediately shows the extra interest burned and the exact drop in annualized `XIRR`.
3. **"Chasing Big Profit Over Fast Wealth" (Capital Efficiency):** Look at two real projects inside the engine:
   - **Project A:** €2.90M profit, requires **€5.80M peak cash** -> **17.8% Levered IRR**
   - **Project B:** €2.50M profit, requires **€3.90M peak cash** -> **21.4% Levered IRR**
   By tracking **Peak Out-of-Pocket Cash**, you instantly spot which property grows your money faster with far less cash at risk.
4. **"Spreadsheet Fatigue & Silent Formula Errors":** Building a reliable 60-month model takes 30–40 hours—and one misdragged `=SUM()` cell can overstate profit by tens of thousands. Here, every formula is pre-built, locked, and audit-checked.

### Modular Formula Reference (100% Native Excel, Zero VBA)

- **Scenario Management Module:** Uses `=XLOOKUP(Active_Scenario, Scenario_Table[Scenario], Scenario_Table[Value])` and `=CHOOSE(Scenario_ID, Base, Optimistic, Downside)` to switch stress-test cases instantaneously.
- **Dynamic Timeline Engine:** Uses `=SEQUENCE(1, Project_Months+1, 0)` and `=EDATE(Project_Start, Month_Index)` to align acquisition, construction draws, lease-up, and loan maturity.
- **Debt & Credit Line Engine:** Supports **Bullet (Interest-Only)**, **French (Constant Payment)**, and **Italian (Constant Principal)** amortization alongside automated **Revolving Credit Facility** sweeps (`=MIN(Funding_Gap, Credit_Facility_Limit)` whenever `Running_Cash_Balance < 0`).
- **Return & Peak-Cash Engine:** Computes exact time-weighted returns via `=XIRR(Cash_Flows, Dates)`, `=NPV(Discount_Rate, Cash_Flows)`, and peak capital exposure via `=ABS(MIN(Cumulative_Equity_Cash_Flow))`.

### Built-In Input Validation & Error Safeguards

| Input Field | Validation Rule | Model Safeguard Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Investment Strategy** | Must match supported strategy list | Invalid selection blocked |
| **Active Scenario** | Base / Optimistic / Downside only | Unrecognized scenario prevented |
| **Purchase Price** | Greater than `0` | Warning alert displayed |
| **Construction Allocation** | Monthly CapEx curve must sum to `100%` | Schedule imbalance flagged on dashboard |
| **Loan-to-Value (LTV)** | Between `0%` and `90%` | High-leverage warning triggered |
| **Senior Interest Rate** | Non-negative percentage (`>= 0%`) | Financing engine paused on negative entry |
| **VAT Recovery Delay** | `0` to `12` months | Out-of-bounds delay resets to default timing |
| **Exit Month** | Must occur strictly after acquisition | Premature exit calculation blocked |
| **Occupancy Rate** | Between `0%` and `100%` | Invalid rental revenue estimate rejected |
| **Mandatory Inputs** | Core assumptions cannot be blank | Dashboard banner switches to **Incomplete Inputs** |
